# 诚信 (Integrity)

Integrity is the virtue of wholeness — acting in alignment with your stated principles, especially when no one is watching. It is not simply honesty in the sense of not lying, but integrity in the sense of being undivided: the same person in public and private, in easy decisions and hard ones, when it benefits you and when it costs you.

The test of integrity is not what you do when the choice is between right and wrong — that is usually obvious. The test is what you do when the choice is between right and convenient, right and profitable, right and popular. Integrity is most expensive when it matters most, which is why it is rare. A person of integrity has a single standard that does not bend with circumstance. The commitments they make, they keep. The principles they state, they follow. The values they claim, they demonstrate.

The long-term advantage of integrity is trust. Trust is the most efficient coordination mechanism humans have. When you are known to act with integrity, people trust you without verification. They take your word as binding. They collaborate with you without contracts. This trust is earned slowly through consistent behavior and lost instantly through a single violation. The cost of maintaining integrity is paid daily in small temptations. The cost of losing it is paid once, in full, and it is almost always higher than you expect.

## Daily Practices

- Make only promises you intend to keep, and keep every promise you make
- When you make a mistake, admit it immediately without deflection
- Act the same way in private as you would if your actions were public
- When a decision conflicts with your principles, name the conflict rather than rationalizing around it
- Practice saying no to things that compromise your integrity, even when saying yes would be easier

## Reflection Questions

- Did I act today in alignment with my stated values?
- When was I tempted to compromise my integrity and what did I choose?
- If my actions today were made public, would I be comfortable with them?
- What commitment have I made that I am not fully keeping?
