# 保身 (Self-Preservation)

Self-preservation is the foundational virtue — the recognition that without your health, all other pursuits are compromised. It encompasses both physical and mental wellbeing: sleep, nutrition, exercise, stress management, emotional regulation, and the maintenance of boundaries that protect your capacity to function over the long term.

The practice of self-preservation is not about avoiding risk or discomfort. It is about ensuring you have the energy, clarity, and resilience to face what comes. A athlete does not preserve their body by avoiding training — they preserve it through intelligent training, recovery, and nutrition. The same applies to the mind: mental preservation is not about avoiding challenge but about managing the load so you can sustain performance over decades rather than burning out in months.

The most common failure of self-preservation is treating the body and mind as infinite resources. You skip sleep to meet a deadline, skip meals because you are busy, skip exercise because you are tired, and skip emotional processing because you do not have time. Each individual skip seems justified. The cumulative effect is diminished capacity across every dimension of life. Self-preservation is the discipline of saying no to the urgent so the essential is not sacrificed.

## Daily Practices

- Prioritize sleep — seven to eight hours is not optional, it is the foundation of cognitive function
- Move your body daily — not necessarily intense exercise, but consistent physical activity
- Practice emotional awareness — notice when stress, anxiety, or frustration is building and address it early
- Set and maintain boundaries between work and rest, obligation and choice
- Schedule regular health check-ins: physical, mental, and emotional

## Reflection Questions

- Did I prioritize my health today, or did I sacrifice it for short-term productivity?
- What am I doing to maintain my physical and mental capacity for the long term?
- Where am I treating my body or mind as an infinite resource?
- What boundary do I need to enforce to protect my wellbeing?
