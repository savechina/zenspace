# Health (保身)

Physical health is the foundation on which all other virtues depend. Without energy, you cannot be diligent. Without sleep, you cannot be thoughtful. Without a body that works, the mind that drives it is compromised. Benjamin Franklin listed temperance first among his virtues, but health is more fundamental: it is the vessel that carries all other efforts. Neglecting health to pursue productivity is like sawing off the branch you are sitting on.

The daily practice of health is not dramatic. It is boring, repetitive, and non-negotiable. Sleep seven to eight hours. Move your body for at least thirty minutes. Eat food that does not come from a box. Drink water. These are not aspirational goals — they are baseline requirements for a functioning human. The challenge is not knowing what to do. The challenge is doing it consistently when the immediate pressure to skip feels urgent. Every skipped workout, every lost hour of sleep, every meal eaten at the keyboard is a withdrawal from a finite account.

The connection between physical health and mental performance is not metaphorical — it is biological. Sleep deprivation impairs judgment as effectively as alcohol intoxication. Chronic stress degrades working memory. Poor nutrition reduces cognitive bandwidth. When you feel foggy, unfocused, or irritable, the first intervention is not a productivity system. It is sleep, food, and movement. The body keeps the score, and it always collects.

## Daily Practices

- Sleep and wake at consistent times, even on weekends
- Move your body for at least thirty minutes — walk, stretch, exercise
- Eat whole foods at regular intervals; avoid eating at your desk as a default
- Take a brief walk or stand up every hour during focused work
- End the work day with a clear transition ritual that signals rest to your body

## Reflection Questions

- Did I sleep enough last night to think clearly today?
- Am I treating my body as a tool to maintain or a resource to exhaust?
- What physical habit, if changed this week, would most improve my daily energy?
