# Virtue Domains

The seven virtue domains form the foundation of personal cultivation in the Zen system. Each virtue represents a dimension of a well-lived life, and together they provide a balanced framework for growth, reflection, and daily practice.

## The Seven Virtues

| Virtue | Name (ZH) | Domain | Core Practice |
|--------|-----------|--------|---------------|
| [Self-Preservation](self-preservation.md) | 保身 | health, wellbeing | Protect physical and mental health first |
| [Knowledge-Seeking](knowledge-seeking.md) | 求知 | learning, growth | Continuously expand knowledge and understanding |
| [Altruism](altruism.md) | 利他 | relationships, service | Serve others, contribute to community |
| [Integrity](integrity.md) | 诚信 | ethics, character | Act with honesty and moral principles |
| [Diligence](diligence.md) | 勤奋 | work, productivity | Consistent effort and hard work |
| [Frugality](frugality.md) | 节俭 | finance, resources | Wise use of resources, avoid waste |
| [Innovation](innovation.md) | 创新 | creativity, problem-solving | Find new solutions, challenge status quo |

## Practice

Each virtue domain includes:
- **Daily Practices**: Concrete actions to embody the virtue
- **Reflection Questions**: Prompts for journaling and self-evaluation
- **Streak Tracking**: Days since last practice in each domain

For deep practice, focus on one virtue per week, cycling through all seven over seven weeks. After the initial cycle, return to the virtues that need the most attention based on your streak data and reflection notes.
