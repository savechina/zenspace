# Resolution (决心)

Resolution is the virtue of doing what you set out to do. It is the bridge between intention and completion. Without resolution, plans remain plans, goals remain goals, and commitments remain aspirations. Resolution means that when you decide to do something, you do it — not perfectly, not without difficulty, but completely. The failure of resolution is not inability. It is abandonment. You had the ability. You chose something easier.

The discipline of resolution requires honest commitment. Most people over-commit and under-deliver because saying yes feels better than saying no. Resolution does not mean saying yes to everything. It means saying yes to the right things and then following through without fail. Before committing, consider carefully. After committing, do not reconsider. The person who constantly re-evaluates their commitments is not being thorough — they are being unreliable.

The practical test of resolution is simple: look at the things you said you would do. How many did you complete? Not how many did you start, or how many did you make progress on — how many did you finish? Completion is the measure of resolution. A project that is 90% done is not done. A habit that is maintained for three weeks and then abandoned is not a habit. Resolution is not about effort — it is about the willingness to see things through past the point where the effort becomes tedious.

## Daily Practices

- Before committing to a task, ask "am I willing to see this through to completion?" — if not, do not commit
- When you notice yourself avoiding a task, do it first — the avoidance is a signal, not a command
- Track your commitments in one place and review them weekly — complete, abandon, or renegotiate each one
- Break large commitments into smaller milestones so completion is visible and motivating
- When you complete something, acknowledge it — completion feeds the habit of resolution

## Reflection Questions

- What did I commit to this week that I did not complete, and why?
- Am I saying yes to things I am not willing to follow through on?
- What is one commitment I have been avoiding, and what would it take to complete it today?
