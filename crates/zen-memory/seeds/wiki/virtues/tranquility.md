# Tranquility (平静)

Tranquility is the virtue of maintaining inner peace in the face of disturbance. It is not the absence of problems — it is the refusal to let problems disturb your capacity to think clearly and act well. Franklin's formulation was "be not disturbed at trifles, or at accidents common or unavoidable." The discipline is in distinguishing between what is within your control and what is not, and then reserving your emotional energy for the former.

This is perhaps the most difficult virtue to practice because it requires ongoing judgment in real time. The provocation is immediate. The response must be chosen in the moment. Unlike diligence or order, which can be planned and structured, tranquility requires presence — the ability to notice your emotional reaction, evaluate it, and choose a response rather than a reaction. This is a skill, not a personality trait. It can be practiced and improved.

The practical application is the Stoic dichotomy of control. When something goes wrong, ask: "Is this within my control?" If it is, act. If it is not, accept. The waste of tranquility is spending emotional energy on things you cannot change — traffic, weather, other people's decisions, past mistakes. Every minute spent being upset about something outside your control is a minute stolen from something within it. This does not mean being passive. It means being strategically active — directing your energy where it can make a difference.

## Daily Practices

- When you feel disturbed, pause and ask: "Is this within my control?" Act or accept accordingly
- Begin each day with five minutes of quiet — meditation, breathing, or simply sitting without input
- When you notice yourself ruminating, redirect your attention to one actionable thing you can do right now
- Limit exposure to sources of unnecessary disturbance — news cycles, social media arguments, other people's drama
- End each day by acknowledging what you controlled well and releasing what you could not control

## Reflection Questions

- What disturbed my peace today, and was it within my control?
- Am I carrying anxiety about something I cannot change, and can I release it?
- What would my day look like if I directed my emotional energy only toward things I can influence?
