# 分不清重点 (Misplaced Priority)

Misplaced priority occurs when the energy and attention invested in a decision does not align with the core pursuit. You spend hours optimizing a detail that matters little while the critical lever sits untouched. This is not laziness — it is misdirection. The brain prefers familiar problems over important ones because familiar problems feel productive even when they are not.

The pattern is seductive because the work itself is real. You are genuinely analyzing, comparing, and deciding. But the decision you are making is not the decision you need to make. You are choosing a font for a product nobody wants. You are optimizing a cost structure for a business with no revenue. The quality of the subordinate decision is irrelevant if it does not advance the core pursuit.

## How It Manifests

- Spending a week comparing cloud providers before validating that customers want the product
- Optimizing tax structure for income that does not yet exist
- Choosing a framework before defining the problem it must solve
- Debating office layout while the sales pipeline is empty

## Warning Signs

- You cannot articulate how this decision advances your core pursuit
- The decision feels important but you cannot trace its impact to an outcome
- You are more comfortable with this decision than with the decision you are avoiding
- Stopping feels like giving up rather than redirecting

## Counter-strategy

- Before any decision, write: "This advances [core pursuit] by [specific mechanism]"
- If you cannot fill in both blanks, you are solving the wrong problem
- Ask: "If I get this perfectly right but everything else stays the same, does anything change?"
- Track the opportunity cost: what critical task is this decision displacing?

- 第一性原理 (First Principles): strip to fundamentals — what truly matters?
- 能力圈 (Competence Circle): stay focused on what you can actually influence
