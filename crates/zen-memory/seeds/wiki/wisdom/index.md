## Using This Library

- **Mental models** provide frameworks for thinking — study them, practice them, add them to your latticework
- **Anti-patterns** are traps to avoid — when you recognize one, pause and apply the counter-strategy
- **Cross-reference**: many models and anti-patterns interact (e.g., Opportunity Cost counters Sunk Cost Fallacy)
